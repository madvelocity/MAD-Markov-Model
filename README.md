# MAD-Markov-Model
An interpretable semi-Markov model of market regimes. Standardized Moving Average Distance (MAD) is binned into six ordered states, and a signal the discrete chain discards — where price sits within its band — forecasts the direction of the next regime transition, calibrated out-of-sample on 26 years of S&amp;P 500 data. Regime state, not returns.
